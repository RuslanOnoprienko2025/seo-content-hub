## WiseStamp Plans and Pricing

WiseStamp currently offers 5 tiers. Although WiseStamp displays its plans as monthly prices, all paid subscriptions are billed annually. In other words, the advertised monthly rate is the equivalent monthly cost, but payment is collected upfront for the full year.

### Free Plan

WiseStamp has a free plan with basic features. It includes a limited signature editor, a small number of templates, and access to some social icons. Most of the features that make WiseStamp useful—custom branding, banner campaigns, multiple templates, team management—are locked behind paid plans.

The free plan works as a trial experience more than a usable long-term option for professionals.

### Pro+ ($9/month)

The entry-level paid plan. Includes one seat and three templates, billed annually ($108/year upfront). Additional seats cost $2 each per month on top of the base price.

This plan makes sense for individuals who want full feature access without team management. Once you add even a few team members, the per-seat costs start changing the math.

### Basic ($19/month)

Includes one admin account and five templates, billed annually ($228/year upfront). Additional seats are $1 each per month. Designed for small teams with basic automation needs.

### Grow ($49/month)

One admin account, five templates, billed annually ($588/year upfront). Additional seats at $1 each per month. Built for growing teams that need more automation capabilities.

### Advanced ($179/month)

Unlimited admins and templates, billed annually ($2,148/year upfront). Volume discounts available. Designed for larger organizations that need full platform access.

### Enterprise

Custom pricing, contact sales required.

## How WiseStamp Billing Actually Works

The plan prices are only part of WiseStamp cost. The per-seat structure on top of each base plan is where the actual number gets calculated.

Here's what different team sizes actually pay annually:

- **1 user (Pro+):** $9/month = $108/year
- **5 users (Pro+):** $9 + (4 × $2) = $17/month = $204/year
- **10 users (Basic):** $19 + (9 × $1) = $28/month = $336/year
- **25 users (Grow):** $49 + (24 × $1) = $73/month = $876/year
- **50 users (Grow):** $49 + (49 × $1) = $98/month = $1,176/year

Two things stand out from these numbers.

First, there is no monthly billing option. Whatever plan you choose, you commit to a full year upfront. If the tool doesn't work out, or if your team size changes, you've already paid.

Second, the per-seat charge stacks on top of the base plan. So the advertised price is always a floor, not a ceiling. The actual cost depends on how many users you need, which plan tier those users require, and which features your team actually uses.

## What Users Say About WiseStamp Pricing

Reviews from G2, Capterra, and GetApp show a split picture.

Some users find the pricing reasonable, especially for small teams that actively use the marketing features. The ability to add banners, campaigns, and social apps through the signature justifies the ongoing cost when those features are actually in use.

Others describe frustration with the billing experience. Common issues include annual charges appearing without clear notification, difficulty finding a cancellation option in the dashboard, and confusion about how seats are counted when team size changes. One Capterra review describes being charged via Visa after believing they had canceled, with support not resolving the issue.

The pricing structure itself also gets mentioned as confusing. Multiple tiers, per-seat add-ons, and annual-only billing create a system where the real cost isn't obvious until you've already committed.

Is WiseStamp worth it for your situation comes down to one question: are you actually going to use the marketing features? If yes, the cost makes sense. If not, you're paying platform prices for a signature.

## Is WiseStamp Worth It?

For teams that actively run campaigns through their signatures—rotating banners, promotional CTAs, centralized updates across 20+ employees—WiseStamp does what it's built for. The annual cost makes sense when the features are in constant use.

For everyone else, the calculation is harder.

A freelancer updating their signature once or twice a year is paying $108 annually for a tool they barely touch. A small team of five paying $204/year for signatures that never change is essentially funding a platform they don't need.

**WiseStamp makes sense if:**

- You treat your email signature as a marketing channel (banners, campaigns, rotating CTAs)
- You manage signatures across a team of 10-25 people and need centralized control
- You actively update signature content for promotions and campaigns

**WiseStamp is harder to justify if:**

- You need a professional signature without marketing features
- You're a freelancer, solo professional or SMB that only updates its signature occasionally
- You want to avoid annual commitments for tools you use infrequently
